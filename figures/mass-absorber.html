MASS Tuned Absorber

MASS · Modular Acoustic Suppression System

Cal Poly NASA HuLC senior project · tunable membrane absorber for ECLSS noise, 60–200 Hz

Illustrative figure. Cavity depths come from eq. 7 and the 0.05–0.205 m travel in the NASA technical paper. The paper gives no damping term, membrane density, actuator speed, controller enclosure or microphone mount, so those are assumed here: absorption uses Rloss = 1.5 ρc in eqs. 9–12, and the membrane mass is fitted to Fig. 4. The planned acoustic test was not run, so the % and dB values are modelled, not measured.
