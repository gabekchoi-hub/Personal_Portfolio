Double pendulum lab

V3

B-2 Spirit mass moment of inertia

Vibrations · Torsion and pendulum methods+
  • Measured the mass moment of inertia of a simple wood board (checked against hand calculations) and then of a B-2 Spirit aircraft model about its roll and yaw axes.
  • Used two experimental methods, torsional vibration and pendulum swing; torsion matched the board’s theoretical values within 2.0% (Y) and 3.3% (Z).
  • Pendulum swing was less accurate (about 19–26% error), most likely from friction and alignment; concluded torsion is the more reliable method for the bomber, whose complex shape has no hand-calculable answer.

Wing structure lab
