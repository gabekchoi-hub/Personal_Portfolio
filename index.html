Adaptive acoustic suppression for Artemis

NASA Human Lander Challenge+

Absorber System Design Lead · Senior design project

  • One of 12 teams selected and awarded $9,000 to develop MASS, an adaptive acoustic-suppression concept for NASA Artemis lander ECLSS equipment.
  • Led the modeling and design of a tunable membrane absorber targeting 60–200 Hz noise, cutting device depth from 0.305 m to 0.205 m while keeping target-frequency coverage.
  • Designed and validated a sensor-integrated actuator that swept 60–250 Hz, equalized chamber pressure in 4.49 s, and survived simulated launch vibration at 10.3 g RMS. Co-authored and presented the NASA technical paper.
