3.2

Modular high-voltage battery integration system

Mechanical Lead · SolidWorks · 3D printing+
  • Designed a modular battery integration system for a high-voltage prototype vehicle, packaging 180 LFP cells in a fixed, space-constrained enclosure. The existing box could not be modified and the cells fit with minimal clearance.
  • Balanced dense packaging, safe electrical integration, thermal insulation, secure retention and a removable pack for off-board charging.
  • Created a stackable 10-cell module with insulation foam between cells, integrated bus bars and custom fire-resistant 3D-printed structural parts.
  • Delrin “waffle” plates at top and bottom locate each cell in X and Y; a clamping lid secures the stack vertically, preventing cell movement under vibration while staying serviceable.
  • Developed the initial full-system concept and CAD (cell layout, insulation strategy, electrical interfaces, retention), then as Mechanical Lead led a mechanical subteam that refined it for manufacturability, assembly and integration.
